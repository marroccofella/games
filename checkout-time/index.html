MEET THE OWNER / ALWAYS OPEN TO OFFERS

Monty Upsell. Still taking viewings.

Lavender suit. Gold briefcase. Absolutely no grasp of the market conditions. Monty follows your porter through the chaos, turning every disaster into a property feature.

“That is not a horde. It is an extremely motivated viewing party.”

Press N or tap Haggle to negotiate. The asking price starts at one bag of biscuits. His sales pitches count separately; nobody buys anything and your rescue score stays yours.

The night shift, explained

You are the porter. Save the guests, contain the outbreak and survive from 23:00 to 06:00. Seven fictional hours take six real minutes. Lead guests east to the glowing green coach boarding ring. Monty will try to include the coach in the sale.

Guests first. Brochures later.

  1. Walk close to a guest to recruit them. Lead your followers to the coach. The green arrow points the way.
  2. A grabbed guest has a shrinking ring. Shoot the grabbing zombie before the timer expires; off-screen arrows show seconds remaining.
  3. Hold at a doorway to barricade it. A sealed zombie contributes less to the outbreak, but can bash the door.
  4. Collect tea and biscuits to restore health. Red extinguisher pickups give foam for crowds in front of you.
  5. Watch red eyes: the zombie is about to swing. Keep moving.

Five stars. Some assembly required.

The final review awards one star for each goal:

  • Reach dawn at 06:00.
  • Save at least 10 guests.
  • Save at least 22 guests.
  • Lock at least 3 zombies in.
  • Reach dawn with no more than 6 guests turned.

The night escalates at midnight, 01:00, 03:00 and 05:00. Bars empty, runners arrive, the sleeping indoor zombies wake, then last call brings a double wave. Monty calls this “a thriving evening economy.”

If it will not start

Use a current browser with JavaScript and WebGL enabled. Click Check in to start sound. Losing focus automatically pauses; click Resume when you return. Use Sound off for silent play. Touch users get on-screen movement and fire controls.

The map and characters use simple 3D shapes. Upper floors are scenery; there is no upstairs gameplay or online co-op. Audio is synthesised locally, with no recorded voices.

No real hotel. No guest register.

The hotel, owner, guests, map and sales jokes are invented. The public game contains no real property floor plans, photographs, correspondence, real transaction details, passwords or private knowledge-base links.

No accounts, analytics, microphone access, cloud audio, ads or in-game purchases. Only your best score is stored in this browser; clear site data to reset it. Game assets are served locally. GitHub Pages, as the host, receives normal page requests.

Run it yourself

Download the source folder and run node server.mjs, then open http://127.0.0.1:8793/. No package installation is needed. Run node check.mjs for logic tests and node privacy-check.mjs for the publication audit. See the full README and MIT licence. Three.js has its own MIT notice.
