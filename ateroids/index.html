🚀 @TEROIDS

Arcade word & number shooter — asteroids that shoot letters and numbers at you.

▶ Play @TEROIDS

What it is

A fast arcade shooter with a twist on the classic: the asteroids raining down are letters and numbers. Pilot your ship, line up your shots, and blast the alphabet out of the sky to rack up word and number scores before they reach you.

Controls

← / →Move your ship
SpaceFire
On-screen buttonsMove & fire on touch devices

Features

  • Word & number scoring — build up your run as you clear the field
  • Keyboard, mouse, and touch controls
  • High score, lives, mute, and pause
  • Fully static and offline — no accounts, no trackers, no external services
