🚀 @TEROIDS

An arcade word shooter: break rocks, collect letters, build words.

▶ Play @TEROIDS

What it is

Rotate and thrust through a wrapping asteroid field. Shooting rocks releases letters; collect them with your ship to form words and earn word scores. Smart bombs clear a crowded field.

Controls

← / →Rotate your ship
↑Thrust
BSmart bomb
SpaceFire
P / EscapePause or resume
On-screen buttonsRotate, thrust & fire on touch devices

Features

  • Asteroid points and collected-letter word scores using the included 39-word starter list
  • Keyboard and on-screen touch controls
  • High score, lives, keyboard and on-screen pause; silent gameplay
  • Fully static and offline — no accounts, no trackers, no external services

Source

Canonical development repository: github.com/marroccofella/ateroids. This copy is vendored into the arcade library so the games live together in one place.
