# FREEL*ADER 42 — all atoms inside the ride

Release: **2026.09.11-full-spin** · 11 September 2026

[Play the latest game](https://marroccofella.github.io/games/freeloader-42/play/?release=2026.09.11-full-spin)

Willie's whole body turns head over heels: his head passes below his feet and continues through complete 360-degree rotations. He makes 4.2 turns while spiralling inward, shrinks into the centre of the asterisk wormhole, and reappears upright at the next property. Both 2D CRT and 3D FIELD share the same choreography and existing layered portal sound.

## Choose portal motion

The title and pause screens now include **PORTAL MOTION**:

- **FULL SPIN** enables the complete rotating, shrinking wormhole effect, including when the device requests reduced motion.
- **GENTLE** keeps Willie upright and full sized, using a gentle fade.
- **DEVICE** follows the device's motion preference and remains the default.

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Other game motion continues to follow the device setting. Previously, a reduced-motion preference could silently suppress the spin even though the latest wormhole release was loaded.

Collect every receipt and enter the glowing asterisk. For a demonstration, enter a property and select **WATCH AI**. Press **P** for the pause screen and **V** to swap 2D and 3D.

## Verification

The game checks cover a visible upside-down pose, a complete rotation while the sprite is still large, arrival upright, gentle mode and saved-preference parsing. Browser checks verify the full-spin choice and persistence on a device reporting reduced motion. Menu and pause panels remain scrollable on short screens.
