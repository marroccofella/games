Two distinctly unreasonable jobs

Bug Hunt: pick up the blaster near the entrance and zap every patrol. Press F, X or FIRE. Guided bolts seek nearby enemies, straight bolts hit anything they cross, and ammunition is unlimited. The portal opens only after the room is clear.
