What it is

An original terminal maze-chomper for the momm universe. You are a green ◆ clearing a maze of evidence dots while four ghosts hunt you — and each ghost is one of momm's review failure statuses, with its own distinct behavior. Grab a power pellet and the tables turn: they go fail-closed and flee, worth escalating combo points if you catch them.

Know your ghosts

TimeoutHunts you directly — relentless pursuit.
Err503Ambushes three tiles ahead of where you're heading.
AuthFailFlanks — mirrors Timeout through your position for a pincer.
TierLoses its nerve — chases until close, then scatters.
